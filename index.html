About me

I am currently a postdoctoral fellow at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, working with Prof. Charles Zhang. I received my Ph.D. from Sun Yat-sen University, where I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Zibin Zheng and Prof. Yuhong Nan. I used to work on software security, building automated testing and analysis tools to find bugs in blockchain systems and compilers. I am currently interested in LLMs for security and software analysis, and LLM security. I used to play CTFs and hunt bugs on Immunefi.
